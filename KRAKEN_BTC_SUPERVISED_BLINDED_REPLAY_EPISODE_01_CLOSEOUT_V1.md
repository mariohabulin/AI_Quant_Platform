## Methodology finding

The episode successfully demonstrated the causal chart reveal, state-valid
action prompts, mandatory contemporaneous reason, durable write-before-advance
ordering and bounded 60-decision completion. It also demonstrated that an
unassisted discretionary human replay is cognitively expensive and leaves key
terms such as confirmation, normal pullback, strong volume and invalidation
insufficiently specified for reproducible strategy construction.

That finding does not say that the BTC decisions were profitable or
unprofitable. No return, P&L, hit rate, benchmark, drawdown, ranking or
parameter comparison was calculated or authorized.

## Closeout decision

Supervised replay v1 is paused after the completed BTC episode. ETH and XRP
remain unopened under the sealed v1 schedule. No additional v1 operator
authorization is granted. The next research boundary is AI-driven v2: explicit
causal measurements first, then a separately reviewed deterministic state
machine, risk plan, next-bar execution and durable decision evidence.

The BTC v1 window is inspected development context and can never be called
unseen validation. The v1 evidence remains immutable and separate from all v2
protocol IDs, schedules and evidence roots.
