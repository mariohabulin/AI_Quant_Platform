# Vision

Build a small, reproducible research platform that rejects weak trading ideas quickly and protects capital when evidence is insufficient.

## Success

A candidate succeeds only when it shows stable, positive, after-cost performance on data it was not allowed to learn from. Otherwise the required action is `HOLD_CASH`.

## Non-negotiable rules

- No live orders without a separate explicit authorization.
- Development, Calibration, and Evaluation remain separated; unopened partitions stay sealed.
- Data, assumptions, costs, and result hashes must be reproducible.
- One cheap falsification comes before new infrastructure or model complexity.
- Closed experiments are not kept in the active tree; Git history is their archive.
- A failed hypothesis is a useful result, not a reason to rename and repeat it.

## Direction

US equities are the preferred next market only if a legal, complete, zero-cost point-in-time source is confirmed. Kraken crypto research is paused after repeated terminal `HOLD_CASH` results.
