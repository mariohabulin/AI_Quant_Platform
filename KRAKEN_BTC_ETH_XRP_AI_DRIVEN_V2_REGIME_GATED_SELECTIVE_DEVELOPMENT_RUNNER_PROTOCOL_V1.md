## Frozen computation

The runner rebuilds the existing paired LONG/SHORT cost-aware outcomes on the
context-complete Development interval. The strict signs of `return_14`,
`ema_12_48_spread` and `ema_180_distance` select LONG, SHORT or neutral before
scoring. Neutral rows remain `HOLD_CASH`. The context variant additionally
requires positive six-bar open-interest change, direction-aligned basis change
and funding inside the frozen non-crowding boundary.

For both variants and both directions in each of the three outer folds, the
earlier 75% of eligible outer-training timestamps fits one natural-frequency
regularized logistic base model. The later 25% fits one sigmoid calibrator.
Event completion is purged before both inner and outer boundaries. The frozen
budget permits at most twelve base-model fits and twelve sigmoid-calibrator
fits. An unsupported direction-fold records a fail-closed support diagnostic
and fits neither artifact for that cell.

Only the base-fit region derives mean positive and mean nonpositive net R. The
action threshold is their payoff break-even probability plus `0.05`, capped at
one. Equality, neutral regime, failed context confirmation, missing class
support or insufficient calibrated probability is `HOLD_CASH`.

## Frozen evidence and gates

The atomic evidence package contains canonical JSON, binary LF SHA-256
sidecars, OOF rows and 24 separately hashed base/calibrator artifacts. The
independent reader hashes every byte and never unpickles a model.
The protocol, runner, review and tests are explicitly checkout-stable LF files
so their static source hashes are identical on Windows and Linux.

Absolute viability requires in every fold at least 30 raw selections, ten
chronological per-asset non-overlapping selections and positive mean plus
cumulative net R. Overall net R must be positive, at least two assets must be
positive, and each direction's calibrated Brier score must strictly beat its
outer-training prevalence forecast in every fold. The context variant must
also beat its matched control in overall mean, worst-fold mean and at least two
fold means. The control may pass absolute Development evidence but does not
prove incremental derivatives value. No passer is selected automatically.

There is no feature, learner, class-weight, parameter or threshold sweep. There
is no top-k rescue, polarity flip, added indicator or automatic successor.
