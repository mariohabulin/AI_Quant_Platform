# Selective Swing Trading Research Mandate v1

## Status

`DECLARED_NOT_EXECUTED`

This mandate resets the active alpha-research direction after the completed
Trend Pullback Volume v1 development screen. It changes research priority and
scope only. It does not create a strategy, acquire a new dataset, execute a
backtest, select a parameter, authorize Candidate v2 or authorize PAPER, cloud
or live trading.

## Decision

The AI Alpha Engine will be developed as a selective swing-trading system that
continuously observes supported markets but normally holds cash. Trading
frequency is not a target. A position may be opened only when a pre-registered
mechanism has a causal, sufficiently complete and cost-aware reason to act.

The research hierarchy is:

1. listed equities are the primary long-term market;
2. cryptoassets remain an active secondary market;
3. daily completed bars are the primary decision resolution;
4. weekly and longer history may provide context but may not leak future data;
5. cash is an explicit valid portfolio state, not a failed selection.

## Reason for the reset

The existing platform has strong deterministic data, validation, execution,
risk, evidence and operational foundations. The closed research programs also
show that the tested standalone indicators, ADX impulse variants and exact
trend-pullback catalog did not produce a robust deployable edge on the frozen
BTC/ETH six-hour development boundary.

Those results do not establish that systematic trading is impossible. They
establish that the tested configurations and research universe were too narrow
to support promotion. The next phase therefore begins with documented strategy
replication and a separately stated price-volume hypothesis instead of another
incremental indicator variation.

All prior code, reports, closures and hashes remain historical evidence. The
previously proposed six-hour pullback trade-path attribution is deferred, not
deleted and not reinterpreted as unseen evidence.

## Research sleeve A — stocks-first documented replication

The primary equity research sleeve will investigate a faithful, testable
representation of the CAN SLIM / O'Neil growth-stock methodology before any AI
enhancement is allowed.

The research definition must distinguish and timestamp every component:

- current quarterly earnings and revenue evidence;
- annual earnings and business-growth evidence;
- a reproducible definition of a new company, product, service, management or
  price catalyst;
- supply-and-demand evidence, including float and causal trading volume;
- relative leadership of the stock and its industry group;
- institutional sponsorship available at the historical decision time;
- broad-market direction and distribution/accumulation state;
- daily/weekly base structure, pivot, breakout and sell rules.

The sleeve is not allowed to reduce CAN SLIM to a generic moving-average or
volume breakout rule while retaining the CAN SLIM name. If a source component
cannot be represented faithfully, the limitation must be explicit and the
result must be named as a derivative hypothesis rather than a replication.

Before performance research, an equity data audit must prove:

- point-in-time fundamentals and original publication timestamps;
- corporate-action handling without future-adjustment leakage;
- historical universe membership, delistings and inactive symbols;
- point-in-time sector and industry classification;
- survivorship-safe price and volume history;
- executable liquidity, spread, commission, tax and currency assumptions;
- a chronological development boundary and a genuinely unseen future boundary.

No stock universe, data vendor, broker or numeric CAN SLIM threshold is frozen
by this mandate. Those decisions require a separately reviewed protocol.

## Research sleeve B — crypto capitulation-volume reversal

The secondary crypto sleeve will formalize the user's prior manual observation
as `Crypto Capitulation-Volume Reversal v1`.

The initial research universe is BTC, ETH and XRP on completed one-day bars.
The hypothesis is that a rare, material decline followed by exceptional volume,
price stabilization and causal confirmation may identify a selective long
swing opportunity. The intended observation is approximately several days to
ten days of holding, but duration is descriptive until the protocol freezes an
exit policy.

The future protocol must separately define:

- prior-decline magnitude and historical context;
- capitulation volume relative to a lagged trailing baseline;
- stabilization and confirmation after, not during, the suspected capitulation;
- following-session executable entry;
- protective stop and position-risk policy;
- high-volume bearish distribution exit;
- exceptional one-day or two-day advance exit;
- momentum-failure and maximum-holding exits;
- liquidity, venue, spread, commission and slippage assumptions.

The first evidence step is a blinded chart-replay workflow. At each decision,
only bars available at that time may be visible. Entry, skip and exit reasons
must be recorded before later bars are revealed. Positive, negative, skipped
and ambiguous examples all belong in the evidence set.

The chart replay is hypothesis reconstruction, not profitability evidence. It
may inform a small pre-registered catalog but may not select a historical
winner or authorize deployment.

## Separation of the two sleeves

The stock and crypto sleeves share infrastructure, not alpha claims.

- CAN SLIM is primarily a growth, leadership and confirmed-strength framework.
- Capitulation-volume reversal is primarily an event-driven recovery framework
  after exceptional weakness.
- Equity fundamentals must never be replaced by crypto proxies while retaining
  the CAN SLIM identity.
- Crypto observations must not influence equity thresholds or vice versa.
- Each sleeve requires its own dataset contract, protocol, evaluation and
  closure before any portfolio combination is studied.

## Role of AI

AI is not authorized to invent or silently mutate entry and exit rules in live
operation. After independently validated strategy sleeves exist, future AI may:

- rank eligible opportunities;
- estimate expected edge net of cost;
- classify market context and confidence;
- allocate risk across strategies and correlated positions;
- recommend or require cash when no eligible opportunity exists;
- detect evidence drift and request offline review.

Model training, feature selection and adaptive allocation require separate
point-in-time data, evaluation and governance contracts.

## Controlled sequence

Work proceeds in this order:

1. integrate this research mandate and reconcile project documentation;
2. audit and acquire the required BTC/ETH/XRP daily research data;
3. implement the blinded crypto chart-replay evidence boundary;
4. pre-register the Crypto Capitulation-Volume Reversal v1 protocol;
5. implement and review its strategy only after the protocol is frozen;
6. audit point-in-time equity/fundamental data for faithful CAN SLIM research;
7. pre-register a CAN SLIM replication protocol;
8. evaluate each sleeve separately on development data;
9. reserve genuinely unseen evidence before any promotion decision;
10. consider AI ranking and portfolio combination only after independent edge
   evidence exists.

Implementation order does not change market priority: the crypto evidence
workflow may be built first because the platform already has relevant market-
data infrastructure, while equities remain the primary long-term market.
